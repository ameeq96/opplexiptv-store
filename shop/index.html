Plan your Opplex equipment purchase carefully

Before buying a new device, separate the costs involved: hardware, any player licence and the service plan. This page is a purchasing checklist, not a claim that we stock a particular product or bundle another company's digital services.

Check what your current device can do

Find its model number and available app store, then check support for the player you want. A software update or a simpler setup may be enough; do not assume new hardware is required.

Review the seller's offer

Confirm warranty, return conditions and any recurring app charges with the relevant seller. Ask Opplex support about compatibility before purchase. Equipment bought elsewhere follows that seller's terms, not the digital-plan refund policy shown here.

Before you buy: a quick device checklist
  • Note your exact device model and operating system.
  • Check the player app and any separate app licence fee.
  • Confirm the selected plan's simultaneous connection allowance.
  • Ask support to confirm your intended setup and location. A device name alone is not a compatibility guarantee.
