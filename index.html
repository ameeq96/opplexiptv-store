Before your first order

New to IPTV?
Start with your setup.

You don't need to know every technical term. A few checks now make it easier to choose an Opplex IPTV subscription that fits how you actually watch.

A player and a subscription aren't the same thing

An IPTV player is the app you use to open a service on your screen. Installing one does not, by itself, provide a subscription or confirm that your chosen plan works with it. Before paying, ask which player suits your device and whether that player has a separate licence or fee.

For a Smart TV, note the brand, model and operating system. For a Fire TV Stick or Android TV box, include the model and the name of any player you already use. That gives support something specific to check, rather than a guess based on the word “TV”.

Choose for the way you use it

One person moving between a phone and a television is not the same as two people watching at once. Check the simultaneous connection allowance on the selected plan. Also confirm any particular content you want before ordering; don't assume it is included because a service supports your device.

What happens when you continue to checkout?

This page gets its plan catalogue from the main Opplex IPTV website. When you choose a package, the central website passes that selection to Opplexify. Review the package and final amount there before continuing to SafePay. Opening checkout does not complete a payment.

Keep your order confirmation or invoice after payment. If something needs checking, the order reference is more useful to support than a screenshot of your bank balance. Never send a card number, password or one-time verification code in chat.

Should I buy a new device first?

Not necessarily. Start by asking about the device you own. Share its model and player app with support, then decide whether you need a different setup. The device list above is a starting point, not a guarantee for every model.

Can I ask a question before choosing a plan?

Yes. Use WhatsApp or the live chat icon on this page. Tell the team you are comparing Opplex IPTV plans and mention your device, how many people will watch at the same time, and anything you need confirmed before buying.
